Every now and then a new font comes out or you just stumble on a new one.

## The serif

- [**Lora**](https://fontsource.org/fonts/lora) is the font this site uses. I found it via [Martin Fowler's](https://martinfowler.com/) blog.
- [**Merryweather**](https://fontsource.org/fonts/merriweather) is the font I use in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/). I found it while trying [Typora](https://typora.io/).

## The sans-serif

- **Atlas Grotesk** is neat, but it has a commercial license. I got used to it in [Dropbox Paper](https://paper.dropbox.com) and I miss it.
- [**Open Sans**](https://fontsource.org/fonts/open-sans) is one I default in Google Docs.

## The monospace

For code my top choices are:

- [**Monaspace**](https://monaspace.githubnext.com/)
- [**Jetbrains Mono**](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/)
